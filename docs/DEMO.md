# Investor demo script · سيناريو العرض

Prepare: open `/admin` on a laptop and the game `/` on a phone or a second tab. Arabic is the default language; the globe button switches to English.

| # | Step | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sign in | `/admin` |
| 2 | Search **الستين** or **Sixty Meter** in the map search and pick the street | City map |
| 3 | **Add business**, then click the map beside the road: the entrance and kerbside pickup snap to the nearest street | City map |
| 4–6 | Enter the Arabic and English names, pick a category and building type, upload a logo (PNG/JPEG/WebP) and watch the **in-game sign preview** | Editor |
| 7 | Adjust the **entrance**, **pickup** and **drop-off** points (drag them, or choose a point and click the map) and add menu items | Editor |
| 8 | **Save changes** | Editor |
| 9–10 | Open the game (or press **View in game**). The building stands at the saved spot with its name and logo on the sign | Game |
| 11 | Tap the building, or the business button that appears when you are near it, to open its card: cover, logo, menu with illustrative prices, missions, hours, distance | Game |
| 12 | Accept a mission (from the card or the job board) | Game |
| 13–14 | Follow the amber route to the pickup, then press **Collect order** in the zone (or **Go inside & collect** for interior pickups) | Game |
| 15–16 | Drive to the drop-off and press **Deliver order** | Game |
| 17 | The results screen shows virtual coins, the on-time bonus, XP, level-ups and achievements | Game |
| 18–20 | Back in the dashboard, rename the business or replace its logo and save. The running game updates within about 6 seconds | Both |
| 21 | Repeat steps 3–8 for a **factory** or **warehouse** (industrial streets west of Sixty Meter Street) | Dashboard |
| 22 | **Sponsorship packages**: edit names, illustrative prices, features and in-game benefits; **Sponsorships**: assign one to a business (a demo action, no payment) | Dashboard |
| 23 | **Analytics**: daily events, a business comparison, demo data clearly labelled; untick "Include demo data" to see only real prototype events | Dashboard |
| 24 | **Demo data**: type `RESET` to restore the original dataset (only works when `DEMO_MODE=true`) | Dashboard |

Talking points that stay accurate:
- The streets are real (OpenStreetMap) for central Erbil: Sixty Meter Street (Kurdistan Street) with its tunnels, Iskan Street, the citadel, the Zanyari towers and Samad restaurant. The demo businesses, prices and analytics history are fictional.
- Package prices are hypothetical. Nothing in the product takes payment or signs a contract.
- Rewards are virtual and cannot be withdrawn.
